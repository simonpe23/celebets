# Upload the Actuals logo files here

Drop them straight into this folder. Nothing else in the repo needs
touching, and nothing is wired to these files until Claude wires it.

## What to upload

Send whatever you have. These are the ones that get used:

| File | Where it ends up |
| --- | --- |
| Wordmark, DARK text | the light theme: header, login page, legal pages |
| Wordmark, WHITE text | the dark theme, same places |
| The A symbol on its own | the favicon, the browser tab, the app icon |

## What makes a file usable

- **Transparent background.** Not white, not black. A background baked
  into the picture renders as a solid box sitting on the page, which is
  what happened with the last set.
- **Two colours of the wordmark**, one dark and one white. The app has
  a light theme and a dark theme, and one file cannot serve both: dark
  text vanishes on the navy, white text vanishes on the white.
- **SVG if you have it.** It stays sharp at any size. Otherwise PNG at
  1000 pixels wide or more.

## The tagline is fine to leave in

Upload the files as they are. Claude crops a no-tagline version.

The tagline only survives at large sizes. At the size the app header
draws the logo, roughly 25 pixels tall, the tagline lands at about 6
pixels and turns to grey mush. So there are two versions of the
wordmark in use: with the tagline on the link preview card, without it
everywhere else.

Worth deciding when you get there: the preview card already carries the
line "Track every bet. Discover patterns. Find your edge." A tagline in
the logo would be a second one in the same image.

## Naming

Do not worry about it. Upload them under whatever names they have and
Claude will rename them.
