## Three things this page does that the new rules forbid

Not bugs to fix tonight. Things whoever briefs Totals has to decide
about, because copying this page copies these.

1. **It shows percentages.** HIT RATE 33%, "55% right (36 of 66)", "41%
   (92 of 227)". The rule since 26 August is records, never percents.
   "36-30" says the same thing as "55% right (36 of 66)" in half the
   space, and does not flatter thin evidence.
2. **The card is called "Sports breakdown" and it lists Crypto.** Crypto
   is Economics, not a sport, ruled 24 August. Either the card is a
   TOPIC breakdown and needs a different name, or Crypto does not
   belong in it. The "Sports only" toggle above it suggests the card is
   showing everything while claiming to show sports.
3. **Odds groups and Singles vs parlays disagree on format.** One writes
   "55% right (36 of 66)", the other "41% (92 of 227)". Same fact, two
   shapes, on one page.
