# Capturing an idea or a parked decision

The owner parks things constantly, and a parked thing that is not
written down is a thing that gets lost or rebuilt.

## Where it goes

`IDEAS.md`, in one of three buckets:

| Bucket | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **NOW** | Small, fits the current app, high value per effort. |
| **SOON** | After the NOW items. |
| **FUTURE** | Big builds, new infrastructure, or business decisions. |

Ask which bucket. **Recommend one if he does not say.**

## What to write

An idea entry is useless without its reasoning. Include:

1. **What it is**, in his words where you have them. Quote him directly.
2. **The date and the trigger.** "Parked by the owner on 24 August 2026
   with 'if it requires too much work now, leave it for later, but dont
   forget'."
3. **Why it is parked**, not just that it is.
4. **The trap**, if there is one. Idea 31 records that swapping one line
   in the picker looks trivial but ships a half-working feature, because
   the category vocabulary behind it is empty for three domains.
5. **The trigger to build it**, where one exists. Idea 30 says: when the
   layouts stop moving.

## The rule that matters

**Ideas are never built from the file alone.** Building always needs a
presented plan and explicit approval. The file is a memory, not a
mandate.

## When he asks to be reminded

Open the entry with a line saying so, so it reads as a reminder when
found later:

> Parked by the owner on 21 August 2026 with "OK for now. But remind me
> to fix this later." THIS IS THAT REMINDER.
